Privacy and AI

What this site keeps, and where AI is used.

A short, plain notice. This is a personal website: no adverts, no tracking cookies and nothing sold. Last updated {{ privacy_updated }}.

Visits

Visits are counted with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and doesn't store IP addresses. It keeps daily totals only: pages viewed, the site you came from, browser, device type, screen width, language and country. The anonymous totals appear on the Pulse page.

Game plays and tool uses are counted the same way: for example, that someone started a game or opened a sample. They are never linked to a person.

The Workbench tools

Analyst on Call: your file is read in your browser and never uploaded. Only a summary of statistics (counts, averages, rates, never your rows) is sent for the AI review.

Model Risk Check: only your answers to the fixed questions, and a model name if you type one, are sent.

Fraud ROI Calculator: runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent.

To keep the free uses fair, the service keeps a scrambled code made from your connection, never the address itself, for up to 30 days. Results are not stored unless you choose to share one (below).

Sharing a result with Ajay

If you keep "Include my full result" ticked when you message Ajay from a tool, that result is saved under a random private link so he can open it. It holds no files, names or contact details, anyone with the link can see it, and it is deleted automatically after 30 days.

Your WhatsApp or LinkedIn message itself goes through those services; this site never sees it.

The Playground Hall of Fame

Adding a score is optional. If you do, the board shows the nickname you choose, your score, the game, the date and your country (worked out from your connection; the address isn't kept). Monthly boards are kept so past champions can be shown.

To count players to date, a scrambled code is kept for each player, never the address. Your nickname is remembered in your own browser for next time.

Where AI is used

{{ site.weekly_name }} articles are drafted by AI agents in a fixed format, then every claim is checked against cited official sources and corrected or removed if it doesn't hold. Each draft waits 48 hours for review before it goes live. The scenario in each article is an illustrative composite, not a real employer or client.

The AI tools use Claude, by Anthropic, under Anthropic's commercial terms, which say Anthropic doesn't train its models on this data. One AI drafts, a second checks, and you see both. Treat every answer as a first read, not advice.

How often the checks corrected or removed something is published on the Pulse page.

Services behind the site

  • GitHub Pages hosts the pages.
  • Cloudflare runs the tools' service, the Hall of Fame and shared results.
  • Anthropic provides the AI.
  • GoatCounter counts visits.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces.

Like any web service, each may keep short technical logs under its own privacy policy.
